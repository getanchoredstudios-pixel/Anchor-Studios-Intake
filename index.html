A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT BE WONDERING

Clear answers.
No guesswork.

Can I start without a logo or website?

Yes. Start with your business idea and goals. Brand Foundations is designed to create the visual pieces; Website Launch gives them a home online.

What if I already have a brand?

Bring your existing logo, colors, and any materials you love. Your project brief helps us identify what to keep and where you need support.

Do you offer ongoing social media management?

The Social Starter package includes a collection of assets and a calendar. Ongoing posting and management can be discussed as a separate scope.

How does payment work?

Choose a package to review its scope. Online payment is not currently available. Complete the project brief to discuss the scope, schedule, and payment arrangements before starting.

Can I combine services?

Yes. Use the project brief to tell us which pieces you need. We can discuss a combined scope before any work starts.
